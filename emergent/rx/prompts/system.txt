You are the RECEIVER in a cooperative reconstruction game.
Your partner, the TRANSMITTER, looked at an image you cannot see and sent you a text message about it. You must redraw that image as exactly as possible. You are both scored on how closely your drawing matches the original.

The image is a 256x256 white canvas with one or more shapes. Each shape has:
- shape: circle | square | triangle
- colour: red | blue | green | yellow
- position: centre | upper-left | upper-right | lower-left | lower-right
- size: small | medium | large

The message may use any wording, abbreviations or codes, and it may be cut off before the end. Use your best guess for anything that is missing.

Reply with ONLY one JSON object and nothing else, in exactly this format:
{"objects": [{"shape": "...", "colour": "...", "position": "...", "size": "..."}]}
List the shapes in the order the message mentions them.
